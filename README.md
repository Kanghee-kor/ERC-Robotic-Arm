# Project Overview
This repsotiry documents the design and development of a 6-degree of freedom robotic arm for the European Rover Challenge (ERC) 2026. 

The robotic arm is designed to perform scientific manipulation tasks during the competition, including object retrieval, sample collection, drilling operations and general manipulation in a simulated Mars environment. 

As the Robotic Arm Team Lead, I was responnsible for leading the complete mechanical development of the arm, from the initial concept through engineering analysis, CAD design, prototype manufacturing and technical documentation.
